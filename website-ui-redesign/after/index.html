02 — Process

How a repair works here

  1. 01

    Drop off

    Tell us what feels off, or nothing at all — we do a full 12-point check either way.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We call before doing anything beyond the original ask. No surprise charges at pickup.

  3. 03

    Repair

    Your mechanic works the whole ticket start to finish — one hand on it, not three.

  4. 04

    Ride

    Every bike gets a lap around the block before it's handed back. If it's not right, it stays.
